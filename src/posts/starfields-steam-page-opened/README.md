## Announcement

Starfield, an open-world game set in space, published and developed by Bethesda, was announced in 2018. We saw its first trailer at E3 2021 in the past months.

One of the most anticipated games of the coming year, Starfield's Steam page has been opened. Unfortunately, we could not learn more details about the game, but you can now add the game to your wish list if you wish. You can access the game's Steam page here. Starfield will release the Xbox Series and PC on November 11, 2022.

It will be available on Xbox Game Pass from day one.
