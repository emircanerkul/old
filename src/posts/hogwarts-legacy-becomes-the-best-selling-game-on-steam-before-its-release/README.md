Pre-orders for the game, which will be released in <strong>one month</strong>, have recently begun on all platforms. Today's information shows that <strong>sales for the PC version are very high</strong>. According to Steam's data, Hogwarts Legacy has become the <strong>best-selling game worldwide before its release</strong>. The game has <strong>topped the charts</strong>, outpacing Call of Duty Modern Warfare 2 and FIFA 23. Only free-to-play games like Apex Legends and Counter-Strike Global Offensive are ahead of Hogwarts Legacy.

<strong>Previously delayed several times</strong>, Hogwarts Legacy is said to be an <strong>open-world game</strong> set in the Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry in the 1800s, <strong>years before the main Harry Potter story</strong>. Players will be able to <strong>create their own characters and embark on adventures in the Harry Potter universe</strong>.
