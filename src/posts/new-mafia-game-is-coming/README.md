A new statement from 2K Games confirms that Mafia developer Hangar 13's Studio Head Haden Blackman and COO Matthew Urban have left the company. But what made these differences significant was the revelation that the new Mafia was in development.

## How will Mafia “Nero” with Unreal Engine 5 look like?

According to the report prepared by Kotaku, the code name of the new Mafia has been determined Nero. The report, which claims that the game has entered the development stage, will have a story that will take place before the Mafia trilogy.

<lazy-load tag="img" :data="{ src: '/images/content/2022/5/new-mafia-game-is-coming-1.png', alt: 'New Mafia game' }" />

Unlike the engine used for Mafia: Definitive Edition, Mafia II: Definitive Edition, and Mafia III, Mafia Nero, which will be developed with Unreal Engine 5, is already intriguing with both its graphics and gameplay.

Hangar 13, the developer of the Mafia series, started other productions before this project but had to cancel them due to some problems. Although official statements about these productions have not been received from the company; It was stated that three games named Rhapsody, Volt, and Mosaic were shelved while they were under development.

After three failed projects, Hangar 13 finally seems to be returning to the more established Mafia franchise. However, let us remind you that there is no official statement and we do not know at what stage the game is.

<lazy-load tag="img" :data="{ src: '/images/content/2022/5/new-mafia-game-is-coming-2.png', alt: 'New Mafia game' }" />

While it is thought that the departure of Hangar 13 COO Matthew Urban and Studio Head Haden Blackman may create a domino effect within the company, the potential of the new Mafia to be affected by this should not be ignored. Still, Mafia with Unreal Engine 5 will manage to attract the attention of players.
