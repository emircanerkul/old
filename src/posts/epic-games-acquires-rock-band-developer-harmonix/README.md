Epic Games Acquires __Harmonix__

According to the latest announcement, Epic Games has acquired video game developer Harmonix, known for its Rock Band series.

After this purchase, Harmonic will continue to support the content of Rock Band 4, while working on a "musical journey and gameplay" for Fortnite.

#### After this purchase, Harmonix also answered some curious questions:

- "We will continue with our current [Rock Band 4] downloadable content plans and complete 2021 and lots of great tracks coming in 2022!"
- There will be no change for FUSER.
- No more instrument plans for Rock Band.
- All of Harmonix's games will continue to be available on Steam and console.
- Harmonix has no plans to change the way it supports any of its older games.
