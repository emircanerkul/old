Riot Games starts a new season every year, with players completing tasks and earning rewards as they progress. One new champion to be added in 2023 is Milio, an Ixtal-originated sorcerer, but no detailed information has been released about this character. Another new champion is Naafiri, a darkin assassin that will be on the mid lane.

## Champions

* Milio
* Naafiri

## Updated champions

The champion Ahri, who has Nine tails fox form, will receive a new appearance. Along with this, the character's skins and visuals will also be updated.
Aurelion Sol will undergo a comprehensive change, and it will have completely renovated gameplay sets.

## How will the LoL 2023 ranked season be?

This year, the ranked season will be reset twice. The reason for this is to reduce stress for players who experience rank decay or demotion in the middle of the year. It should be noted that rewards will also accompany the reset."


## When the second season begins in League of Legends?

The reset will not be at the same level as the reset at the beginning of the year. The effect of the reset will depend on how many games players have played since the start of the season. If you have played fewer games, the effect will be lower.

In addition, players will no longer have to climb up to Gold rank to win the ranked costume during the new season. You will be able to win the costume once you reach the number of games determined by your rank.
