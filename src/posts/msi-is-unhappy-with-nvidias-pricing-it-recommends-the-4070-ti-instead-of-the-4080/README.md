## MSI recommends the RTX 4070 Ti

With the introduction of the RTX 4070 Ti, MSI made a humorous criticism of Nvidia. MSI announced on social media that it was selling GeForce RTX 4070 Ti cards and made a reference to the price of the RTX 4080 and Nvidia's general pricing policy.

<lazy-load tag="img" :data="{ src: '/images/content/2023/1/msi-is-unhappy-with-nvidias-pricing-it-recommends-the-4070-ti-instead-of-the-4080-1.png', alt: 'MSI is unhappy with Nvidia\'s pricing: it recommends the 4070 Ti instead of the 4080' }" />

Although MSI's attention-grabbing criticism was quickly deleted from social media, it was supported by many gamers. While the RTX 4080 is a performance monster of a graphics unit, the <strong>recommended price of $1,200</strong> for the product has caused serious criticism. The RTX 4070 Ti, supported by technologies such as DLSS 3 and Frame Generation and a beast for 1440p, stands out as a <strong>much more accessible product at a price tag of $799</strong>. While the RTX 4070 Ti is sold at a more affordable price, the AMD RX 7000 series appears to be a <strong>more affordable purchase for many gamers</strong>. It remains to be seen whether Nvidia will make any <strong>price cuts on the 4000 series cards</strong>
