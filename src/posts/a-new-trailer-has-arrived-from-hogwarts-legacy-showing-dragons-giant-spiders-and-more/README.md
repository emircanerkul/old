Hogwarts Legacy, which Harry Potter fans have been waiting for for years, is finally set to be released next month, after many delays. The game, which is available for pre-order on all platforms, has already proven its popularity even before its release. In fact, last week it managed to surpass games like Modern Warfare 2 and FIFA 23 and become the <strong>best-selling game on Steam.</strong>

Today, an <strong>important development</strong> for the game that will allow fans to experience J.K. Rowling's universe in the way they imagined it, has occurred. The official YouTube account for Hogwarts Legacy has released the official <strong>cinematic trailer</strong> for the game.

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NthGfn_ddRQ' }" />

The new trailer, which includes dragons, enemies, and a Weasley, gives us an idea of what we can expect to encounter in Hogwarts Legacy. The trailer highlights many details, from the details of Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry to the enemies that players will fight. In addition, we can also see that a <strong>dragon</strong> will also be included in the game.

We also hear the phrase "Professor Weasley" in the trailer, which signals that a member of the Weasley family from the main Harry Potter series will appear in the game as a <strong>professor at Hogwarts</strong>. Given that the game is believed to take place in the 1800s, we can speculate that this character could be Ron's <strong>great-grandmother</strong>. In addition, students fighting spiders and using forbidden curses are also exciting parts of the trailer.

The game, in which players can create their own characters and embark on adventures at Hogwarts, will be released on <strong>February 10, 2023</strong>.
