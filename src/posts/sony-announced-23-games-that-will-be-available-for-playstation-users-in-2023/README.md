Let's take a look together at what PlayStation owners will play this year.

## 23 games for PlayStation owners in 2023

### Marvel’s Spider-Man 2

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qIQ3xNqkVC4' }" />

> Release date: 2023

Marvel's Spider-Man, released by Insomniac Games in 2018, and Marvel's Spider-Man: Miles Morales, released in 2020, received a lot of praise from players. The continuation game of the series was announced at the E3 fair in 2021. It has been confirmed that the game will be released in 2023, but the exact release date is not yet known.


### Horizon Call of The Mountain

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q5NdLcyKM98' }" />

> Release date: February 22, 2023

Horizon Zero Dawn, released in 2017, and Horizon Forbidden West, released last year, received positive reactions from many players and were even loved. In recent months, Sony also surprisingly announced the new VR game of the series, Horizon Call of The Mountain. The bad news is that you need to have a PSVR to play the game.

### Suicide Squad: Kill The Justice League

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_vDpMejzar4' }" />

> Release date: Spring 2023

After the disappointment of the 2016 film, Warner Bros. made Suicide Squad fans angry with a similar production in 2021. The game was repeatedly postponed. Most recently, it was announced that the postponed game will be released in the spring of 2023.

### Hogwarts Legacy

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fHoqULb8Qbs' }" />

> Release date: February 10, 2021

Hogwarts Legacy, announced in 2021 and eagerly awaited since then, is currently ranked first among the most anticipated games on both Steam and the PlayStation Store.

### Resident Evil 4

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E69tKrfEQag' }" />

> Release date: March 24, 2023

The remaster of Resident Evil 4, one of the most beloved productions of the Resident Evil series, was announced at the recent State of Play event. The game features the beloved character Leon.

### Assassin’s Creed Mirage

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x55lAlFtXmw' }" />

> Release date: Fall 2023

Assassin's Creed Mirage, which was leaked a few months ago and then officially announced, is set in an alternate universe where the Templars won the war against the Assassins. The game, which was developed by the creators of the Far Cry series, is expected to be released in the fall of 2023.


### SEASON: A Letter to The Future

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ERJgyk5My5o' }" />

> Release Date: January 31, 2023

SEASON, which stands out with its different language of expression and graphics, is one of the most eagerly awaited games of 2023, although we are almost certain that those who play it will love it.

### STAR WARS™ Jedi: Survivor

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VRaobDJjiec' }" />

> Release Date: March 17, 2023

Jedi Survivor, the sequel to Star Wars Jedi: Fallen Order, which caused a great stir with its first game and is considered the best Star Wars game in recent years, continues the story from where it left off and presents us with the adventures of Cal Kestis, who is trying to escape the Imperial Order.

### Final Fantasy XVI

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wvunu2e29tc' }" />

> Release Date: June 22, 2023

Final Fantasy XVI, the last game in the Final Fantasy series, which was first released in 1987 and has been a phenomenon since then, is coming back to shatter sales records again.

### Stellar Blade

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ayek3ZzWb1E' }" />

> Release Date: 2023

Stellar Blade, a game originally announced in 2019 as Project EVE but later changed its name, is one of the most eagerly awaited JRPGs of 2023.

### Tchia

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L3cXwsJLefg' }" />

> Release Date: 2023

Tchia, a physics-based sandbox game, has already proved to us that it will be a very different game with its structure that allows you to control all the objects and animals around you and its ability to play real ukulele.

### Wild Hearts

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CKJW0P6DVGM' }" />

> Release Date: February 17, 2023

Wild Hearts, which stands out with its Monster Hunter World and Fortnite hybrid structure, is a very interesting game that wants you to hunt much larger monsters than you by building Fortnite-like structures and using your skills.

### Destiny 2: Lightfall

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lfoeZLp5A7k' }" />

> Release Date: February 28, 2023

Lightfall, the new expansion pack for Destiny 2, takes the war between the Soliders of The Shadow Legion and the Guardians one step further.

### Firewall Ultra

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pqSJRTTiTtM' }" />

> Release Date: 2023

Firewall Ultra, the sequel to the tactical VR FPS shooter game Firewall, which was released for the PS4 VR, stands out from its rivals as the most advanced tactical VR game so far.

### Dead Space Remake

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cTDJNZ9cK1w' }" />

> Release Date: January 27, 2023

Dead Space Remake, the remake of the space horror TPS game Dead Space, which attracted attention worldwide at one time, is coming back as confidently as the first game.

### Forspoken

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9Ph8D4MkGiI' }" />

> Release date: January 24, 2023

Inspired by Harry Potter and other fantastical universes, Forspoken appears to be one of the most attention-grabbing games of 2023, with its unique fighting mechanics and extraordinary enemies.

### Street Fighter 6

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U747IQXEoto' }" />

> Release date: June 6, 2023

The new game from one of the world's most beloved fighting game series, SF6, invites arcade fighting enthusiasts to combo up at their PlayStation this year.

### Eternights

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YkN-2gUUmc8' }" />

> Release date: 2023

An "relationship action game" in an anime style, Eternights is a game that those familiar with the Persona series are sure to love. The game has three main rules: gather materials, fight a lot, and occasionally meet up with your sweetheart.

### Horizon Forbidden West: Burning Shores

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ShJuY6Zl2Aw' }" />

> Release date: April 19, 2023

The expansion pack for Horizon Forbidden West, Burning Shores, pits Aloy against even more dangerous beasts in the future of Los Angeles.

### SYNDUALITY

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5Cd5PcLDh9I' }" />

> Release Date: 2023

A JRPG, Sony's favorite genre, SYNDUALITY leaves Magus and his artificial intelligence partner in the midst of robots and acid rain in a post-apocalyptic world.

### Lords of The Fallen

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uOJA9tFV5Qw' }" />

> Release Date: 2023

A sequel to the 2014 game of the same name, Lords of The Fallen invites players to a dark and fantastical realm.

### Pacific Drive

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dN1I_znpzrk' }" />

> Release Date: 2023

In this FPS survival game set in the US, you try to escape from an abandoned town in your car and flee from the fantastical creatures that come your way.

### Alone in the Dark

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=I0WPDDn6DpY' }" />

> Release Date: 2023

A remake of the 1992 game of the same name, this game returns to the theme of 90's cult horror films, instead of the disappointment of the 2008 game with the same name.
