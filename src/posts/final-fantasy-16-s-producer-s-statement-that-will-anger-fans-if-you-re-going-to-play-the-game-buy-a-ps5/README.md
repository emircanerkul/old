Final Fantasy 16, announced at a PS5 event in 2020, also created a lot of excitement like the other games in the series. But this year, the producer made a statement that angered PC players when the <strong>release date was confirmed.</strong>

Although all trailers for FF 16 have indicated that the game will be exclusive to the PlayStation, the text below the trailers says <strong>"Also available on PC."</strong> Additionally, officials from Square Enix, the game's producer and distributor, have repeatedly said that the <strong>game will be released first on PlayStation consoles and then available for sale on PC a few months later.</strong> Even a DualSense controller advertisement from PlayStation in Japan clearly stated that the <strong>game would be exclusive to the PS5 for only the first 6 months.</strong>

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ofWtkPs92Nc' }" />

During the Final Fantasy 14 New Year Tournament in Japan, the game's producer, Naoki Yoshida (Yoshi-P) was asked when the game will be released on PC, he replied with "<strong>No one said the game would be released on PC.</strong> Why would you think the game would come out on PC six months later? Go buy a PlayStation 5."

Of course, it is not surprising that <strong>Sony and Square Enix, both based in Japan, support each other.</strong> Additionally, many popular games in Japan are <strong>only available on PS5.</strong> The main reason for this is that <strong>Japanese gamers primarily play on consoles, rather than purchasing Xbox or PC.</strong> However, when it comes to games like Final Fantasy which is <strong>world-renowned and expected by millions of players,</strong> it is expected that a company like Square Enix will release the game on PC to make players happy.
