Sometimes you don't want to spend hours on a game. That's why we've listed 10 long-lasting games that you won't be able to put down.

**Note**: The completion times for the following games are taken from [HowLongToBeat](https://howlongtobeat.com/?utm_source=elitegamers-net). Depending on your play style, these times may be even longer.

## One of the legends of the era: Mass Effect Legendary Edition

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_n8i53TtQ6IQ' }" />

> Completion Time: Main story 59 hours - %100 135 hours

Mass Effect Legendary Edition is a combined version of some of the most beloved action RPGs of all time, the Mass Effect trilogy. The main story of the three games takes a total of 59 hours to complete, while bringing peace to the galaxy by completing all side missions takes about 135 hours.

## An endless chain of monsters: Monster Hunter World

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_OotQrKEqe94' }" />

> Completion Time: Main story 48 hours - %100 393 hours

In Monster Hunter World, you can hunt monsters for hours alone or with friends. If you want to hunt all the monsters and get everything out of the game, you need to spend 393 hours on it.

## Somehow it was nominated for "Game of the Year": Xenoblade Chronicles 3

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Fwuw3PB7Hrc' }" />

> Completion Time: Main story 61 hours - %100 168 hours

Xenoblade Chronicles 3, one of the most controversial games at The Game Awards, was somehow nominated for Game of the Year. This game, which has a very small player base outside of Asia, is one of the games that JRPG fans will love to play for hours.

## Step by step towards becoming a knight in the Middle Ages: Kingdom Come: Deliverence

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_w9IrhaJKwBY' }" />

> Completion Time: Main story 41 hours - %100 129 hours

In Kingdom Come: Deliverance, you start out as the son of a simple blacksmith in the Middle Ages (but you're not really) and suddenly become the lord of your village after your village is attacked. You then try to save your country and become a honorable knight. Kingdom Come is a game that you will be able to play without getting bored, especially with its fighting mechanics and RPG elements.

## The Elder Scrolls III: Morrowind

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_Cr3TCWPlDrw' }" />

> Completion Time: Main story 44 hours - %100 331 hours

If you enjoyed Skyrim and want to play something similar, then Morrowind is definitely for you. The game was released in 2002, but it has one of the largest open worlds to date. While it was a very advanced game for its time, if you are someone who can solve optimization problems with mods and don't care about graphics, you should definitely give Morrowind a chance.

## Spend long hours with dinosaurs and robot dinosaurs: ARK: Survival Evolved

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_5fIAPcVdZO8' }" />

> Completion Time: Main story 55.5 hours - %100 524 hours

Released in 2017, ARK is an unusual game that drops you in the middle of nowhere on an island full of dinosaurs and asks you to survive. If you want to beat the game solo, you need to set aside 524 hours for dinosaurs.

## The longest-lasting game to date: World of Tanks: Blitz

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_FsgyWcvGh6g' }" />

> Completion Time: %100 3600 hours

Yes, you read that right, you need to set aside 3600 hours to completely finish World of Tanks: Blitz. During this time, you have to play all the tanks on all the maps in the game and unlock all the tank's attachments. Of course, we don't know why anyone would spend 150 days playing a PvP tank fighting game to completely finish it.
