Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition, the remastered version of the popular series Grand Theft Auto, published by Rockstar Games, made its debut recently.

Unfortunately, after the game's release, it received vast criticism from the players. On Metacritic, the review score section of the game opened for users. In the past hours, the players gave their points. We can say that the scores are much lower than expected. Currently, the average of 954 reviews is 1. In general, we can say that the reason for the criticism is that the game is getting weird rather than being renewed.

Grand Theft Auto: The Trilogy – The Definitive Edition is currently available for PC, Xbox Series, Xbox One, PlayStation 5, and PlayStation 4. You can find the images shared and widely discuss on social media for the game in the gallery section below.
