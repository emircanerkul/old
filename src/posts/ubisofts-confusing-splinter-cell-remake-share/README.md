Along with the announcement, a photo of Sam Fisher was shared for his 20th anniversary. Now, a post that could be considered a development has been shared from Ubisoft's official Belgian account.

The Splinter Cell Remake development team at Ubisoft has said that they embrace secrecy about the game's development process, but that they are moving with <strong>great care</strong> to develop gameplay mechanics that will satisfy players. Therefore, we have not yet seen any concrete images, videos, or information about the game beyond concept art. The fact that the image shared on the 20th anniversary of the game was re-shared on the Ubisoft Belgian account yesterday is seen by commentators as a <strong>sign that information will be shared about the game soon</strong>.

Perhaps there is information circulating that we may see a small teaser or a new image. Players are also eager to get information about another remake that Ubisoft has left us in great curiosity, <strong>Prince of Persia: Sands of Time</strong>.
