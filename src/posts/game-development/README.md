## Introduce

Developing game is one of the hardest development areas. Game development is not just like web development. Web developers only need some programming languages and just sketch web design. With these tools, web developers can make great sites. But in a game development area, things do not seem like.

## Are you a scenarist?
If you wanna develop a game with a big one-man team you have to be a scenarist too and not a regular one, you have to be a good scenarist. Game scenarios are the most important thing in this field. If your game is really good with all corners like sound, graphics, controls but no scenario, Your players just play your game for just a couple of hours and they will cant hold.

> You have to give something to your players to create a bridge between players and your game. When you create this connection you're game able to stand.

## What about graphics?

But all is not just finished with scenarist like as I told beginning. In these times, games are really really have good quality seems like real. These high standards take players from pixel art games. Yes, there are players and lovers of pixel art-style games but it's limited. If you wanna connect with more people you need to think about that. I don't say your graphic quality must be epicgames standards, just low-poly style is enough to represent your idea without losing much more people with the pixel art style.

You might not good at graphics. If you can use a blender or any three-dimensional design program, you can create low-poly style graphics and that would be a good start but if you already know about pixel art design you can go this way. But if you don't know both, I think the blender is the best option and easy to learn rather than pixel art style graphic design.

## I can hear you.

If you read this article from here, I need to say; Did you play a game without sound?

NOOOO! Sounds have to be there to catch the attention of the player. Without sound, creating a connection between the player and your game would be harder and harder. Without sound, players lose their attention easily and can't focus and dive deep into your game.

## Is your game playable without any hassle?

Of course, bugs are bugs. Players wanna play a game without a problem. And there are lots of profiles of gamers. Gamers are might be younger or older people. When thinking that, you need to focus on some demographic area. And you need to know your target people's habits and skills. If you give your great game to the wrong audience your game just dies without being born.

> Something is valuable only next to someone who knows the value of that thing.
