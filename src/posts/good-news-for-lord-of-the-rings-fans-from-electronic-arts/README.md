As smartphone technology develops, the popularity of mobile games has started to increase in recent years. Electronic Arts also made an attempt towards this very goal. The company announced that it will release a mobile game called The Lord of the Rings: Heroes of Middle-earth, which Lord of the Rings fans are eagerly waiting for.

> Electronic Arts(EA) has partnered with Middle-earth Enterprises, a division of The Saul Zaentz Company, to develop a new system. The Lord of the Rings RPG mobile game will be completely free. Electronic Arts(EA) is expanding its mobile game collection.

On the other hand, Electronic Arts will be available to players with a limited beta version in **some regions** this summer. The Lord of the Rings mobile game is rumored to be inspired by The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit. Detailed explanations about the game are expected to be made soon.
