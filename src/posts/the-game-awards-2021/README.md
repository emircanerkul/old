The Game Awards' nominees for 2021 have been announced. We can say that 2021, which is normally expected to be quite busy, has been one of the driest years of recent years after the postponed games. However, all of the nominated plays are productions that deserve the award.

The best of the year will be selected with the ceremony to do on __December 8__. If you want, you can make your own decisions from the page [here](https://www.thegameawards.com/nominees).

Here are __The Game Awards 2021__ nominees:

- It Takes Two (Hazelight / Electronic Arts)
