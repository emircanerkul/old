The iconic science fiction horror series Dead Space is set to return on January 27th with a new game. The remastered version of the first game, Dead Space Remake, is offering a special pre-order opportunity. EA Games will give the customers who purchase the Remake Dead Space 2 for free.

## Dead Space Remake buyers will get Dead Space 2 for free

Electronic Arts announced that players who pre-order the Dead Space Remake, <strong> which is set to release on January 27th, will receive Dead Space 2 for free. This campaign, which is specially offered to users who pre-order from Steam, makes the 129 TL game free.</strong>

<lazy-load tag="iframe" :data="{ src: 'https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cTDJNZ9cK1w' }" />

For those who are not familiar with the science fiction series, <strong> the game follows engineer Isaac Clarke as he investigates a mining ship filled with mutated crewmates.</strong> The Remake promises <strong>better graphics, sound, and shorter loading times.</strong>

Users who pre-order the Remake on Steam will be able to receive Dead Space 2 for free. <strong>However, it should be noted that a similar campaign will not be valid for players on PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X/S.</strong>

## Dead Space Remake system requirements:

* Operating System: Windows 10 64-bit
* Processor: Ryzen 5 2600x, Intel Core i5 8600
* Memory: 16 GB RAM
* Graphics Card: AMD RX 5700, Nvidia GTX 1070
* DirectX: Version 12
* Storage: 50 GB available space
